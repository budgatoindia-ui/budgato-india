WhatsApp outage troubleshooting in India

How do I check if WhatsApp is down across India right now?

Check this live status and recent community reports, then test another website or messaging service. A local mobile data or broadband issue can look like a WhatsApp outage.

Why are my WhatsApp messages stuck on one tick in India?

The recipient may be offline, your connection may be unstable, or WhatsApp could be experiencing a temporary issue. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and check the service status before resending repeatedly.

What can I try if WhatsApp calls are not connecting on Jio or Airtel?

Confirm data connectivity and app permissions, restart WhatsApp, and try a different network if available. If calls and messages fail for many people nearby, check for a wider service incident.
