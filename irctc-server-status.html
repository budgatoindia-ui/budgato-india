IRCTC outage troubleshooting in India

How can I check if the IRCTC website or Rail Connect app is down today?

Check this live status and reports, try the official IRCTC website and Rail Connect app separately, and consult official IRCTC notices. Heavy Tatkal booking demand can cause temporary slowness.

What should I do if IRCTC Tatkal booking is stuck or payment is pending?

Check your booking history and payment status before attempting another payment. Keep the transaction reference, allow pending payments time to update, and contact your bank or IRCTC through official support if money was debited.

Why does IRCTC show a session or captcha error during Indian train booking?

High traffic, expired sessions, browser cookies, or a connectivity interruption can trigger errors. Sign in through the official site or app, refresh the session, and retry without sharing account credentials or OTPs.
