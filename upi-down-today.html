UPI outage troubleshooting in India

How do I know if UPI or NPCI servers are down across India today?

Review the live status and recent reports here, then check your bank or UPI app and official NPCI or bank notices. A disruption may affect only one bank, app, or region.

What should I do when a UPI payment is pending or fails in India?

Do not immediately repeat a debit that is still pending. Check transaction history in your UPI app and bank account, save the UTR or reference number, and follow your bank's dispute or support instructions.

Why does UPI say bank server unavailable even when my internet works?

The issuing or receiving bank, UPI app, or payment network may be temporarily busy. Wait before retrying, verify the selected bank account, and contact your bank with the transaction reference if money was debited.
